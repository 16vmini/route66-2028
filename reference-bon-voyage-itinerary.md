## Notable omissions / things they skip
- **Tulsa, OK** — Golden Driller, Cain's Ballroom, big Route 66 town
- **Williams, AZ** — most preserved Route 66 town, Grand Canyon Railway gateway
- **Seligman, AZ** — birthplace of the Historic Route 66 preservation movement (Angel Delgadillo)
- **Oatman, AZ** — ghost town with wild burros on the original alignment
- **Kingman, AZ** — Route 66 Museum
- **Hackberry General Store, Roy's Motel & Cafe (Amboy, CA)** — iconic photo stops
- **Calico Ghost Town** — mentioned but only drive-through

## Routing notes
- Santa Fe is on the **pre-1937 alignment** (the original loop). Including it is the "scenic" choice.
- Vegas is **not** on Route 66 — it's a tourist detour. Doing it skips the most authentic AZ→CA desert stretch (Oatman, Needles, Amboy). Trade-off, not a mistake.
- Day 3 covers ~300 miles (Chicago → St Louis with Route 66 stops) — that's a long first driving day after picking up the rental.

## Hotel notes
- **Wigwam Motel (Holbrook)** — genuinely iconic, sleep-in-a-concrete-teepee. Book early.
- **Big Texan (Amarillo)** — the 72oz steak challenge place. Kitsch but iconic.
- **Holiday Inn The Squire** is in **Tusayan** (outside the park). Inside-park alternatives are far more atmospheric: **El Tovar** (historic, on the rim, books ~13 months out), **Bright Angel Lodge**, **Yavapai Lodge**.
- **Inn of the Governors (Santa Fe)** — solid mid-range; for milestone-trip wow, **La Fonda on the Plaza** or **Inn and Spa at Loretto** are the upgrades.
- **Royal Sonesta River North (Chicago)** — fine business hotel; for views consider **LondonHouse** or **The Langham**.
